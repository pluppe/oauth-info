Privacy policy

This app is a private tool used only by the household that runs it. It is not offered to the public.

  • What it accesses: the household's own Google account, with read-only access to Gmail and access to Google Calendar, only after the account owner signs in and approves it.
  • What it does with it: finds dates and reminders in the household's own mail and adds them to the household's own calendar.
  • Where data is kept: on the household's own server at home. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Google API data is used in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revoking access: at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.
